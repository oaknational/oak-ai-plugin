## Oak's curriculum principles

The `oak-curriculum-principles` and `oak-curriculum-principles-mcp-enabled`
skills set out Oak National Academy's six curriculum principles and its
subject-specific guiding principles, as published by Oak:

> Emma McCrea, "Our 6 principles guiding our approach to curriculum", Oak
> National Academy, 5 March 2023.
> <https://www.thenational.academy/blog/our-approach-to-curriculum>

The research these principles draw on — including work by the Education
Endowment Foundation, the Department for Education and Ofsted — is cited in
full in the `oak-curriculum-principles` skill's `references/sources.md` and
`references/principles-in-depth.md`.

## WCAG 2.2

The `oak-accessibility` skill is guidance synthesised from the
[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/),
a W3C Recommendation, with supporting GOV.UK guidance. Its sources are listed in
`references/sources.md`.
