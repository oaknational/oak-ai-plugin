| I want to…                                           | Go to                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Report a problem with a plugin or its answers        | [AI plugin feedback form](https://survey.hsforms.com/2vy6BnIvzTASqx1DbH8CaJAbvumd)        |

GitHub issues and discussions are turned off on this repository. The feedback
form is the route that reaches the team.

## Who maintains this

Triage is owned by the **@oaknational/devs** team at Oak National Academy.

## What to expect

- **First response: within 5 working days.** That is an acknowledgement that a
  human has read your report and decided what happens next — not a fix.
- We do not commit to a fix timescale, and priorities are set against Oak's roadmap.
- Oak works to UK term times. Responses over school holidays may be slower.

## What is in scope

**In scope**

- The plugins published in this repository — how they install, load and
  behave, and the answers their skills and workflows give.
- The accuracy of curriculum information the plugins return from the Oak
  Curriculum MCP.
- The documentation in this repository.

**Out of scope**

- Help with your own AI assistant, account or subscription, beyond installing
  and using the plugin. See your assistant's own help pages.
- Oak's teaching content itself, the main Oak website, or Oak accounts — use
  [support.thenational.academy](https://support.thenational.academy/).
- Guaranteed availability of the Oak Curriculum MCP, or support for plugin
  versions other than the latest. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for which
  versions receive fixes.
- External pull requests. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
